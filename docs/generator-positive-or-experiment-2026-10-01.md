relay and again at its public output tap. An independent physical prototype
shows that pure positive sums can share a passive junction after one isolated
repeater per input. A public literal output must remain on the primary side
of the junction isolation: placing it on the sum rail backpowers its sibling.

The generator recognizes only positive literal terms and constants from the
cover, requiring at least one two-input sum. Each sum input has one independent
repeater. Each requested public literal retains its own separate repeater
and rail. Sum aliases share the certified junction but keep distinct lamp
terminals; literal aliases likewise retain distinct terminals. Constant
domains stay isolated. Mixed polarities and other covers continue through
their existing qualified generator. No source names or function encodings
participate in selection.
