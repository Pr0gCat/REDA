# Comparator inverter world isolation

Parent: `c945a93`. ExpandedStrict Coupling now checks the emitted comparator cell after structural authority and exact emission. Coupling semantic version increases from 3 to 4; the legacy compatibility policy is unchanged.

Using structural potential outputs independent of current power/lit values, the check rejects any potential driver into the constant torch support, any foreign strong driver into the declared side support, and any strong driver into an unused solid side support. The unused side may be air or an electrically isolated solid. The declared source remains the floor lever selected by the structural contract, the constant torch is the structurally owned rear source, and exact emission fixes those block states. Directional sources pointing away are not treated as injecting power.

Negative fixtures include a currently off lever powering the constant support, an unpowered repeater aimed at the declared side support, an unpowered repeater aimed at the unused side support, and dust above the constant support. All reject. The isolated reference geometry and a repeater pointing away pass the isolation helper. These are focused world checks, not complete certified comparator candidates.

Validation: 16 durable physical-verifier tests, 10 revision tests and 4 player generator regressions pass. Comparator Boolean semantics remain unsupported; no ROM layout is changed. Next work is conditional topology semantics plus a complete candidate whose rear and side connections, observations and routes pass the existing strict certifier. Frozen 4096-table quality comparison and deterministic replay are still required before changing generation.
